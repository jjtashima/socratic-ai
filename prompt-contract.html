Build a Prompt Contract

Four questions about your own work. Your answers become a working Socratic prompt you can paste into any AI.

Step 1 of 6

Before you start

A prompt is a block of written instructions you paste into an AI before anyone starts typing. The student never sees it. This page writes one for you.

What happens here

You answer four questions. Nothing is sent anywhere — everything stays in your browser. At the end you get text you can copy.

It takes about ten minutes. There are no wrong answers, and you can change anything afterwards.

Question 1 — the moment

Name one moment in your work where someone should be reasoning, and often is not.

A worked example

You ask someone why they chose what they chose, and the answer is not wrong. It is just empty.

Question 2 — the answer you want

Write the answer you would be thrilled to get. Be specific. If you cannot write it down, the AI has nothing to aim at.

A worked example

“I picked this one because I have actually watched it go wrong. My sister did exactly that and it cost her a semester.”

Question 3 — what you actually get

Write the three answers you get instead. Not the worst ones — the most common ones. The three you could predict before you opened the assignment.

Worked examples

“Because it is important.” — a reason with no reasoning in it.

A restatement of the question in different words.

“I don’t know, what do you think?”

Question 4 — what you would ask next

For each answer you just wrote, one question you would ask next. Just the next question — not a plan, not a lesson.

Worked examples

For “because it is important” — “Important to who? Name one person.”

For a restatement — “You told me what it says. What do you think about it?”

For “I don’t know” — “Forget the assignment for a second. What would you tell a friend?”

Your Prompt Contract

Copy this, paste it into any AI, and start the conversation underneath it. Then run it on yourself, answering the way your most reluctant student would.
